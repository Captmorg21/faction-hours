What is this?

A faction member timezone tracker, made by manhat. Linking up with people scattered across other timezones is a pain, and working out whose evening lines up with whose lunch break shouldn't take a spreadsheet. This is my attempt at solving that.

Mark the hours you're usually about, and the board works out the rest for everyone at once.

Why is this a thing

Chains die because nobody was around to keep them alive. Events get scheduled for the middle of somebody's night. And when a war lands, the hard part is working out which hours are best for everyone. None of that has to be guesswork.

If everyone marks their usual hours, this board shows exactly how many of us are online for every hour of the week, in Torn time, with all the timezone maths already done. Chains get started when there are enough people awake to feed them rather than dying at 40. Wars turn up when they turn up, but once one does, you can see at a glance which hours we've got the numbers to push and which ones need somebody to set an alarm.

It takes a minute, and it's worth doing to help the faction be the best it can be. Finding out that one person is the only cover for a whole stretch of the week is exactly the kind of thing worth knowing before a war, not during one.

We know real life gets in the way. If your availability changes, come back and update it here as soon as you can. A board that's out of date is worse than no board at all. And if you can't make an event you're down for, have a word with faction management rather than leaving it.

Send your hours to the faction board

You need the passphrase your faction leader gave you. The API key is your choice. You can send without it.

Adding a key is worth doing if you're happy to. It locks your entry to you, so nobody else can overwrite your hours. Without it, anyone who has the passphrase could submit hours under your name. Either way your hours go on the board and count towards coverage exactly the same.

How do I get a Public API key, and is it safe?
  1. In Torn, open Settings, then the API Keys tab.
  2. Give the new key a name you'll recognise later, such as Faction Hours.
  3. Set the access level to Public Only. That's the lowest of the four levels, and it's all this site needs.
  4. Create the key, then copy the 16 characters it gives you and paste them into the box above.

A Public key exposes only what anyone can already see by looking at your profile. It cannot show your money, your items, your battle stats, your logs or anything else private. Those need Minimal, Limited or Full keys, and this site will never ask you for one.

No Torn API key of any level can log into your account or change a single thing, because the API only ever reads. Your key holds nothing about your password.

Your key is sent once, used to read your player ID, and then thrown away. It is never written to disk on this server.
